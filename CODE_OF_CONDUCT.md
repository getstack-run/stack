# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a place where people can do careful technical work together, whoever they are and wherever they come from.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person. Assume good faith, and say plainly when you do not.
- Be specific and honest. Say what you tried, what you saw and what you expected. Say "I don't know" when you don't.
- Welcome newcomers. Explain; do not gatekeep. A question is not an imposition.
- Give credit. Name the people whose work you build on.
- Keep security reports private until they are fixed (see SECURITY.md).

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs or demeaning language, whatever the target.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, stalking, threats, or sharing private information about a person without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, spam, or promotion unrelated to the project.
- Pressuring a maintainer or contributor to act outside the project's process.

## Where this applies

In every space of the project: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place where you represent it.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for applying this code. They may remove comments, close or lock threads, reject contributions, and ban people from the project's spaces, temporarily or for good, and they will explain a decision when they can without exposing the person who reported.

Report a problem privately to support@getstack.run. Reports are treated in confidence. If the report is about a maintainer, say so, and it will be handled without involving that person.

## Changes

Anyone may propose a change to this code by pull request; the maintainers decide.
